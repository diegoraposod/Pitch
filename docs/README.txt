PITCH · by Diego Raposo
Drone and binaural generator that tunes itself to the sound around you.
Audio Unit + VST3 · macOS 11 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

INSTALL
1. Double-click install.command.
   If macOS says it can't verify it: click Done, open System Settings >
   Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to
   install.command. Then double-click it again and choose Open.
2. In Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on Audio Units / VST3 and click Rescan.
3. Find PITCH under Plug-Ins and drop it on an audio track.
